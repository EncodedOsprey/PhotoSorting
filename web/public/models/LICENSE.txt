Eye-state and sunglasses classifiers used by PhotoSorting's photo analysis
(downloaded into this folder by web/scripts/prepare-assets.mjs, unmodified):

ocec_s.onnx  OCEC: open/closed eyes classification, by Katsuya Hyodo (PINTO0309).
             https://github.com/PINTO0309/OCEC (doi:10.5281/zenodo.17505461)
             Trained with the Open and Closed Eyes Dataset by Michał Młodawski,
             https://huggingface.co/datasets/MichalMlodawski/closed-open-eyes
             (Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0).

sgc_s.onnx   SGC: sunglasses classification, by Katsuya Hyodo (PINTO0309).
             https://github.com/PINTO0309/SGC (doi:10.5281/zenodo.20824676)
